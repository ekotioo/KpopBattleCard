Game Tutorial

🎮 Getting Started

Welcome to Battle Of Idol! Start by opening your first free gacha crate in the Store to get your first idol card.

🃏 Equipping Cards

Go to the Collection screen and equip your card to use it in battles. You can only use one card at a time.

⚔️ Battling

In the Battle screen, press START BATTLE to begin. The battle is automatic, but you can use shields and attack boosts during the fight.

✨ Skills

Buy skills in the Store and equip them to your cards in the Collection screen. Each skill provides unique advantages in battle.

🏆 Quests & Rewards

Complete quests in the Quests screen to earn coins, cards, and other rewards. Check both Main and Daily quests!

🎁 Gacha System

Open crates in the Store to get new idol cards. Higher rarity crates have better chances for rare cards. Use Luck Boosts to increase your chances!

💰 Economy

• Gacha cards (ID 1-74) can be sold
• Store cards (ID 100+) cannot be sold
• Use discount cards for better prices
• Complete quests for free coins
