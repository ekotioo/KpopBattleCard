Game Tutorial

Getting Started

Welcome to Battle Of Idol! Start by opening your first free gacha crate in the Store to get your first idol card.

Equipping Cards

Go to the Collection screen and equip your card to use it in battles.

Battling

In the Battle screen, press START BATTLE to begin. The battle is automatic, but you can use shields and attack boosts during the fight.

Skills

Buy skills in the Store and equip them to your cards in the Collection screen. Each skill provides unique advantages in battle.

Quests & Rewards

Complete quests in the Quests screen to earn coins, cards, and other rewards.

Gacha System

Open crates in the Store to get new idol cards. Higher rarity crates have better chances for rare cards.

Luck Boosts

Buy Luck Boosts in the Store to increase your chances of getting rare cards from gacha crates. Each boost lasts for 20 gacha openings.
